Process

How a search moves from brief to shortlist

A consistent six-stage process, used on every search to keep quality and turnaround predictable.

Understand

Understand the role, company requirements, ideal candidate profile, location, experience, and hiring goals.

02

Research

Source

Qualify

Review candidate backgrounds, experience, skills, location, and overall fit against the requirements.

Outreach

Contact qualified candidates using personalized and professional messaging.

06
